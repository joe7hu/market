# Product goals: Market

## Purpose

Help one investor understand what changed, identify and compare evidence-backed
opportunities, choose an expression and risk budget, and learn from prospective
paper outcomes. Seek attractive risk-adjusted returns while making uncertainty,
costs, invalidation and downside visible. Activity, high model confidence, a
backtest, or a rising simulated equity curve alone is not success.

## User-facing contract

**Today** answers what requires attention now: what changed, the decision or
blocker, the exact next step, evidence dates, and a working destination. It is
not a log viewer. “Review the evidence” or “plan unavailable” without naming the
missing facts and recovery action is insufficient.

**Market** connects prices, breadth, macro conditions, events and news to the
current regime, horizon, scenarios and competing explanations. Prices follow
exchange sessions; research evolves outside trading hours. Market closure must
not erase a valid last-session price or silently stop news/event refreshes.

**Opportunities** compares research cases and expression choices (stock, option,
spread, cash-secured put, supported crypto/hedge, or cash). A paper-qualified
plan needs explicit entry, size, maximum loss, invalidation, exit horizon,
portfolio fit and traceable decision identity. A research idea can be useful
without meeting execution requirements. Historical terms are not current orders.

**Real portfolio** and **Paper trading** share risk concepts but never balances,
fills, or accounting identities. Paper trading exposes staging, pending reasons,
partial entry, holding, exit, fees, source marks, NAV and evidence gaps. A closed
exchange, untriggered limit or missing quote can legitimately produce no fill.
Research/shadow observations must not manufacture funded paper-account P&L.

**Research** makes strategies and prediction learning inspectable: hypotheses,
versioned factors and parameters, prospective experiments, calibration,
out-of-sample results, failures and promotion decisions. Promote only when
independent deterministic evidence and risk gates pass. Do not optimize a
strategy merely to increase its apparent win rate or number of completed trades.

## Quality bar

Every recommendation or blocked action has one current authority and enough
provenance to explain its inputs, policy, portfolio context and alternative.
Missing values are named gaps, never synthetic zeros. A successful refresh is
not evidence that the underlying price, forecast or source observation changed.

Numerical terms and portfolio effects are computed by deterministic owners.
Language models interpret unstructured evidence and explain results; they do
not invent prices, probabilities, source coverage, risk authorization or fills.
Explanations must not conceal unavailable facts behind confident prose.

Improvement is judged by reconciliation, source and publication coverage,
prospective calibration, net outcomes after realistic costs, risk budget
adherence and the investor's ability to act or abstain for a clear reason.
The system must also be easy for an agent to maintain: one substantive owner
per rule, typed boundaries, focused tests, no competing docs or façade layers.

## Non-goals and safety boundaries

Market is not a guaranteed-profit machine, full exchange/order-book emulator,
live execution broker, or replacement for every charting platform. Current paper
stock fills use a labelled last-price model; option fills use displayed sides
and available-size gates. Neither implies a real broker would fill identically.
The optional WebMCP adapter is for trusted, read-only inspection—not bypassing
backend policy or exposing the workstation to arbitrary external agents.

[Architecture](../ARCHITECTURE.md) owns implementation boundaries. Dated audit
and campaign documents record changes and verification, not competing product
or architecture contracts.
