# Mandatory Broad-Universe Discovery Engine — Compact

Independently scan a broad, liquid US equity/ETF optionable universe plus major liquid crypto assets so supplied names do not become an attention trap. Supplied portfolio, watchlist, and radar names are inputs—not the discovery universe.

Report the universe/provider, scan date, core liquidity/optionability filters, and approximate survivor count. Use primary evidence for catalysts. Rank candidates on catalyst timing, expectations gap, valuation or token economics, technical/flow confirmation, option liquidity, portfolio diversification, consensus saturation, countercase, and bounded payoff.

Return no more than three finalists, clearly marked `INDEPENDENTLY DISCOVERED`, and compare them directly with the best supplied candidate and cash/no-action. Reject crowded, already-priced, illiquid, stale, or weakly sourced setups. Do not manufacture candidates when none clear the bar.
